Schedule

Two days, 9:00 to 17:00, with a 60-minute lunch and two 15-minute breaks. Each block pairs a short talk (slides) with the level's exercises. Times assume a class of up to 16 with prerequisites done before day 1.

The last three columns say who uses them:

  • For learners — Workbook: the level learners work through in the workbook. Share these links with the class.
  • For the instructor — Slides and Demos: the deck at the block's first slide, and what you show on the projector from the instructor demo workbook.
